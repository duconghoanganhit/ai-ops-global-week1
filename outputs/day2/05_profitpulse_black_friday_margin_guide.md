### Post #3 — `r/smallbusiness` (Broader E-Commerce Holiday Margin Lesson)
```text
Title: The hidden reason busy Black Friday weeks sometimes produce less profit than an average Tuesday

When small makers and online shops run steep Black Friday percentage-off promos (25–35%), fixed fulfillment costs (boxes, postage, payment fixed fees, and marketplace ad attribution fees of 12–15%) don't shrink with the sale price.

A quick fix before Friday: instead of cutting price across the board, switch to tiered cart offers (e.g., "15% off orders over $60" or "Bundle 3 for $X"). Customers still feel a clear holiday deal, while your fixed shipping and processing overhead spreads across a larger basket — keeping net margin 10–15 percentage points higher.
```
